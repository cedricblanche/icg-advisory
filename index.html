01 / ABOUT ICG

A clear path from
opportunity to execution.

We bring commercial strategy, Shariah advisory coordination and practical delivery together.

ICG Advisory is the training and consultancy division of ICG-EVENTS Ltd. Based in London, we support financial institutions, businesses, investors and professionals working in ethical and Islamic finance.

Each assignment starts with your objectives. We bring together the financial, legal, Shariah and technical input needed to develop a workable product, transaction or operating model.

02 / OUR EXPERTISE

The expertise to
move your plans forward.

Focused support at every stage, from product design and governance to financing, market entry and delivery.

01

Shariah governance &
product development

Build Shariah requirements into your products, documentation and day-to-day operations.

  • Shariah scholar identification, board appointments and advisory coordination.
  • Institutional operating models, governance policies and approval workflows.
  • Financing and investment product design, documentation and transaction flows.
  • Operating manuals, accounting advice and systems implementation support.
  • Ongoing reviews, audit coordination, screening and specialist guidance on purification and Zakat.

Formal Shariah opinions and approvals are issued by the appointed scholars.

02

Corporate finance &
investment structuring

Assess opportunities, define funding needs and prepare transactions with a clear commercial structure.

  • Business cases, financial planning, due diligence and risk management.
  • Equity, acquisition and bridge finance, syndications, trade finance and asset leasing.
  • Direct investments, joint ventures, fund structures and public-private partnerships.
  • Sukuk preparation, asset and cash-flow assessment, and adviser coordination.
  • Information memoranda, investor materials, rating and listing advice coordination, and post-closing support.

Regulated execution activities are undertaken by appropriately authorised providers where required.

03

Market strategy &
business development

Connect a well-defined offer with the right clients, distribution channels and commercial partners.

  • Market analysis, customer insight and product positioning.
  • Marketing plans, launch preparation and performance measurement.
  • International alliances, introductions and support for partner discussions.
  • Open architecture platforms and assessment of external Islamic investment solutions.
  • Business plans, company presentations and project marketing materials.
04

Training &
people development

Give your teams the knowledge and practical capabilities to implement Islamic finance solutions.

  • Customised programmes for leadership, product and operational teams.
  • Islamic finance principles, governance, products, operations and Sukuk.
  • Practical case studies, documentation reviews and relevant standards.
  • In-house, on-site and online delivery with implementation follow-up.
  • Role scoping, talent identification, skills assessment and management development.
05

Technical advisory &
project delivery

Connect financial and commercial advice with the specialist technical requirements of your project.

  • Feasibility studies, technology assessments and technical due diligence.
  • Coordination of conceptual, front-end and detailed engineering.
  • Procurement planning, tender preparation and proposal evaluation.
  • Contract management, construction reviews and commissioning support.
  • Engineering and project management coordination for energy, environmental and infrastructure assignments.

Technical and engineering services are coordinated through suitably qualified specialists.
